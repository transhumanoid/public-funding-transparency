# Sample Conversation: The Funding Allocation Dialogue

This is the raw exchange that seeded the whole concept. Kept verbatim as a conversation starter — not as scripture.

---

**On power:**

> Power corrupts, absolute power corrupts absolutely. We're no different. We're just subjected to the possibility of a corrupt intern or someone getting in there or someone fucking with it. We're already gonna put the safety mechanisms in place to ensure that we flag it before it happens and if it does happen, then we shut it down. Like straight away. We don't drag it out in some sort of inquiry that lasts for 10 years. We shut it down like in real time.

**On the dashboard:**

> You have the public visualization dashboard with the encryption tied into the flow of money and information and real-time processes happening on the screen that anyone can log into, anyone can see.

**On crypto as baseline:**

> Tying it all into the cryptocurrency is like the baseline minimum requirement. The whole ledger, the tracking, the tracing, like the immutable nature of the recording and the tracking and the tracing of every single dollar in circulation. That's the only way moving forward, really.

**On multi-chain approval:**

> If you had multi-chain, multi-authoring kind of segregated processes of approval, and even members of the community potentially having to sign off on it, then you get around that because you don't need to rush through any payments when you think about it realistically.

**On BSB binding:**

> You could only move it from one authored account or one approved account to another. Someone claiming community funds and the money going somewhere else just won't cut it. A local primary school claiming money for a playground upgrade has the BSB and the account number for the primary school and it has to match.

**On automatic flagging:**

> You almost get it to the point where people don't even have to lodge a complaint. It's there in plain sight. The system's automatically doing that. Automatically running partial investigations and triggers and audit trails. If there's a mismatch, it's already been flagged before it even happens.

**On nudge paths:**

> You could push it or prompt it or nudge it. You can have an email come through officially from the head of the school, the headmaster, the chief of accounts. You can have it getting flagged by members of the community.

**On keys:**

> Every person could have a key. The head of financials, the individuals, the teachers.

**On urgency:**

> The average strike usually only lasts a couple of days. A playground having delayed funding for an extra week isn't critical. It's not like you're talking about medical supplies.

**On NDIS:**

> You only have to look at what the NDIS stuff has cost society, the pink batts schemers, all the different scams and schemes. None of that was even alerted to. Even if it was, they dragged the heels for 6 to 12 months, and by then the accounts were empty.

**On existing tech:**

> You have the auth layer and you also have what the ATO have and you use the existing technology. Export QuickBooks files, use them with internet banking protocols. A condition of this is that you need to upload your recent financials or your recent transaction summary. Use the existing technology.
