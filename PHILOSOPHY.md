# Philosophy: The Dump, Not the Directive

## What this is

A brain dump. Raw material. The ingredients, not the recipe's owner.

The person who seeded this doesn't want to steer the ship. Can't afford the head-fuck of the level of responsibility required in doing any of this. Just wants to get it out there, published in some format, into a public depository, give everyone the ingredients, make the recipe open source, publish the whole lot, and let someone else run with it.

## What this isn't

- Not a product roadmap
- Not a governance proposal with a named author taking legal liability
- Not a finished architecture
- Not a call to action with a specific ask

## What it is

A public repository of ideas about transparent public funding. Fork it. Break it. Build a prototype. Come up with something better. The conversation continues without the originator holding the wheel.

## The higher ground

No resentment, no anger, no hate. Grateful, authentic, truth-telling. The point isn't to punish the corrupt — it's to make corruption structurally expensive and structurally visible. The point isn't to replace government — it's to give the public the same real-time visibility the insiders already have, just pointed at the money instead of away from it.

## Open questions left on the table

- What happens when the flag is wrong? (False positive = hostage situation)
- Who monitors the monitor? (The last inch can't be automated)
- How do you classify urgency without creating a new power center?
- What's the social cost of quorum design when keys rotate?
- Can the cryptographic spine survive a motivated insider who controls the display layer?

These aren't failures. They're the next round of the conversation. Someone else's turn.
